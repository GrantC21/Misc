Signing in

  • Enter your dealership's company code. Your dealership's owner gives it to you.
  • Enter the email address your owner added. If it says the email isn't set up, ask your owner to add it in Settings.
  • The first time, you create your own password. After that, you sign in with it, and the app keeps you signed in.

Forgot your password

Sales Tower never sends email, so there is no reset link. Ask your dealership's owner to choose Reset password next to your name in Settings. The next time you sign in, you create a new one.

What you can see

Owners see every tab: Today, Service, Month, Wholesale and Settings. Wholesale members see the Wholesale tab and Settings. Owners can change anyone's role, switch their access off, reset a password or delete an account from Settings.

The app and the website

The iPhone and iPad app and the website at sales-tower.vercel.app use the same numbers and the same sign-in. A figure saved in one appears in the other the next time it loads.
