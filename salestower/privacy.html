Privacy Policy

Sales Tower · Last updated October 24, 2026

Sales Tower keeps a dealership's daily sales, service and wholesale numbers. It stores only what you and your dealership type into it, uses your email only to sign you in, and never sells or shares your information for advertising.

Who can use it

Sales Tower is a business tool. You can only sign in if your dealership's owner has added your email address and given you the dealership's company code. There is no public sign-up.

Information about you

  • Email address. Used only to identify you when you sign in. Sales Tower never sends you email: no newsletters, no notifications, no password links.
  • Name and role. Entered by your dealership's owner so the right people see the right screens.
  • Password. Stored by our sign-in provider in a protected, one-way form. Nobody at Sales Tower can read it.

Dealership information

The figures you enter belong to your dealership: daily new and used sales and plans, gross assumptions and goals, service and parts totals, wholesale vehicles (stock number, buyer and net amount), buyer names, and store names, colours and logos. Only signed-in members of your dealership can see them, and each person sees only what their role allows. Another dealership can never see your dealership's figures.

Where it is stored

Your account and your dealership's figures are stored with Supabase, a hosted database in the United States, protected by access rules that check your dealership and role on every request. The Sales Tower website is hosted by Vercel. On your iPhone or iPad, the app keeps your sign-in in the device's Keychain, plus a few display preferences such as the last tab you used.

The contact form

If you use "New here?" to ask about Sales Tower for your own dealership, the name, dealership, email, phone number and message you type are sent through Formspree to the developer, only so we can reply to you.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The Sales Tower website uses Vercel Web Analytics to count page visits. It uses no cookies, does not identify you, and never sees anything you type; it records only which pages are opened and the general type of browser and device. The iPhone and iPad app contains no analytics. There is no advertising and no tracking, and your information is never sold, rented or shared for marketing. It is shared only with the service providers named above, and only to run Sales Tower.

Keeping and deleting your information

You can delete your own account at any time: open Settings in the app and choose Delete my account. Your sign-in is removed straight away. Two exceptions keep a dealership from being locked out by accident: if you are its only owner, make someone else an owner first; if you are the last person in it, the app sends us a request and we delete your account and close the dealership for you within 30 days. Your dealership's owner can also remove your access or delete your account from Settings, and you can always ask by emailing the address below.

Your dealership's figures belong to the dealership and stay with it when a person leaves. A dealership that stops using Sales Tower can ask for all of its information to be deleted.

Children

Sales Tower is a tool for dealership staff and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy
