A Guide to Becoming Greater

Finding your way around

The Home tab shows today's reminder, where you left off, and the six steps of the Road to the Sale. The Playbook tab is the full text. Tap the chapter name at the top of the Playbook tab to jump to any chapter or section.

Highlights, underlines, and notes

  • Press and hold any text, drag the handles to cover what you want, then choose Highlight, Underline, Note, or Favorite.
  • Tap an existing highlight to change its color, add or edit a note, share it, or remove it.
  • Everything you save is in the Saved tab, grouped by chapter. The share button there exports all your highlights and notes as text.

Bookmarks and favorites

Tap the bookmark button at the top of the Playbook tab to save your place in a section. Tap the star on any word track or quote to add it to Favorites.

Word tracks with your name filled in

Open Settings (the gear on the Home tab) and enter your name, dealership, title, and phone number. When you copy a word track, placeholders like [Your Name] and [Dealership] are replaced with your details automatically. This information stays on your device.

Search

The search button searches the entire playbook, the glossary, and your own notes. Tap a result to jump straight to that spot. Sections of the playbook also appear in iPhone Spotlight search.

Read progress and reading time

A paragraph counts as read once it has been on screen long enough to read it. Reading time only counts while you are actively reading. Your progress is shown at the bottom of the Home tab. To start over, go to Settings and choose Reset Reading Progress. Highlights, notes, bookmarks, and favorites are kept.

Text size and dark mode

Tap the AA button in the Playbook tab to change text size, font, line spacing, and light or dark appearance.

Updates to the playbook

The playbook is a living document. New and revised content arrives through regular App Store updates at no extra cost.
