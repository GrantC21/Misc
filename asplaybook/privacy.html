Automotive Sales Playbook

Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

Automotive Sales Playbook does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no servers, and no third-party services in the app.

Data you create

Your highlights, underlines, notes, bookmarks, favorites, reading progress, reading time, and the name, dealership, title, phone, and email you can optionally enter in Settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to the developer or anyone else.

Spotlight search

The app adds the playbook's section titles and text to your device's Spotlight index so you can search it from the Home Screen. This index stays on your device. Your highlights and notes are not added to it.

Copying and sharing

When you copy a word track or share a highlight, the text goes only where you choose to paste or send it.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Children

The app is intended for adults working in sales and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Remove individual highlights, notes, bookmarks, and favorites from the Saved tab. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
