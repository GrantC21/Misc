Privacy Policy

Ace: Volleyball Tracker · Last updated August 8, 2026

Ace: Volleyball Tracker does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. There is no server, no account, and no analytics.

Data you create

Player names, jersey numbers, positions, team names, opponent names, game scores, and statistics you enter are stored locally on your device. They are never sent to the developer or to any third party.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your data syncs across your own Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit private database. That data lives in your personal iCloud account. The developer has no access to it and cannot read it. Apple's handling of that storage is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

If you are not signed in to iCloud, the app works normally and keeps everything on your device.

Photos

If you add a photo to a player profile, it is stored with your app data on your device and in your own iCloud account. It is not uploaded anywhere else. The app requests photo access only at the moment you choose to add a photo.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking technologies, no advertising identifier, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

Children

The app is designed for tracking youth sports statistics and is often used by parents. It collects no data from anyone, including children. No information is transmitted off the device by the app under any circumstances.

Deleting your data

The Settings screen includes options to reset an individual player's data or to erase all data in the app. Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device. Data synced to iCloud can be removed through your device's iCloud settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
