Ace: Volleyball Tracker

Support and help

Getting started

Launch the app and follow the short setup to add a player and a team. Both steps can be skipped, and anything you skip can be added later from the Account tab.

Logging a game

Go to the Play tab and tap Start a New Game. Pick an opponent, or tap the + to add a new one.

  • Tap either half of the scoreboard to score a point.
  • Tap a stat tile to add one.
  • Press and hold a tile or scoreboard half for about half a second to subtract one.
  • The small undo buttons reverse the last score or the last stat separately, so fixing a stat can never cost you a point.

Choosing which stats you track

Open Settings from the gear icon on the Account tab, then Tracked Stats. Turn stats on or off, drag them into the order you want, or add your own. The first stat in the list becomes the top-left box on the game dashboard, and the grid fills left to right.

Serve percentage

Each serve is logged as exactly one of three things: Serves In, Aces, or Missed Serves. The app adds those together to work out the total attempts and the percentage that made it over. Renaming any of those three stats will stop the serve percentage from showing.

Seasons, tournaments, and games

  • A game can sit under a season, under a tournament, or on its own.
  • A tournament can sit inside a season or stand alone.
  • A game filed under nothing still counts toward career stats.
  • You can move a game to a different season or tournament at any time from its Edit screen, and its stats move with it.

Tracking more than one player

Tap the player card at the top of the Account tab to switch players or add another. Every player keeps their own teams, seasons, and games, and switching swaps all of it at once.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your data syncs automatically across your own Apple devices. It is stored in your personal iCloud account and is private to you. The app works fully offline if you are not signed in.

Deleting your data

Settings has two options at the bottom: Reset Player Data clears one player's history but keeps the player, and Erase All Data removes everything. Both ask for confirmation first.
